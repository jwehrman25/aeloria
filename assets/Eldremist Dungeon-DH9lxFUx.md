[[The Little Guys]] meet at [[Polb Schtuk]]'s house in [[Gobbo-by-the-Plains]], answering the call of [[Shovel]]. Schtuk, however, never received a letter, surprised by the sudden arrival of [[Chadius the Swole]] and [[Smegenald Dickberries]]. The three discuss what it may be that Shovel called them together for, as Schtuk shows of his new home, with 75% roof coverage and an entirely missing back wall. Eventually, Shovel arrives, attempting to make a grand entrance, but failing to do so. Shovel claims to have gotten reliable information on the location of the woman responsible for killing their former comrade [[Skeech †]]. This rallies the group back together after their 6 month apart, departing for the small and mysterious village of [[Eldremist]]. Chadius remains in [[Rivergate]] though, as his new role as Baron has made it too difficult for him to travel far from his new home.

While on the road, the party notices a broken down cart lying on the side of the road. As Schtuk goes in to investigate, Shovel's familiar notices the presence of [[The Court of Mages]], as if they are looking around for something. Schtuk notices on the cart a broken cage, as if something or someone had escaped. From underneath the cart, an invisible [[Sevrin Blackwell]] attempts to threaten Schtuk into giving him supplies. but the stubborn Goblin attempts to use the Testicular Torsion, which bounces back, but fizzles before doing any damage. This altercation alerts the members of the Court nearby, and combat ensues.

After defeating the Court members Sevrin is revealed as a decaying man, cursed by some sort of magic that is killing him slowly. He joins the party as they happen to be headed to Eldremist, which is his destination as well. While stopped for the night on the road, the group is attacked by Skaven assassins who seem to be after Shovel, who is spooked and honored that he is important enough to have assassins sent after. 

Come day, the group arrives near Eldremist, and true to it's name, a powerful mist surrounded them as they traveled closer to the town. Shovel recognized this as a sort of barrier magic, meant to keep the uninvited out of the town. The Little Guys debated heading back for Eldretop, in order to get an invite from [[Selene Nightshade]], but before the cart could be turned around, Sevrin revealed that he had been given an invite, and extended it to the rest of the party.

Once through the barrier, a group of Eldremist mages rode out to meet the Little Guys, one being [[Atticus]], the town elder (who had a fancier wizard hat than Shovel). He questioned how the group managed to get through the barrier, and upon seeing Sevrin, he recognized the man that [[Elana]], his daughter had described to him. He offered the party to come and meet him at his house, to discus matters.

At the Elder's home, Shovel wasted no time to reveal his desire to kill Elana, showing a chart of how production has dropped since Skeech's death. Atticus challenged this, and tried to seek out a compromise, understanding the pain that his daughter may have caused to him, but would not willingly give up his daughter's life. He then told them that this was all moot for now, as he had not heard from Elana in a few days, as she was sent to the local magical dungeon to perform a ritual in order to maintain the barrier around the town. Atticus offered to the party all spoils found in the dungeon, as well as a promise to get a fancier hat made for Shovel.

Before heading to the dungeon, Sevrin revealed something to Schtuk. Sevrin was previously a part of [[The Court of Mages]], but after some experiments that failed, he was left cursed and seen as nothing but a beast to continue experiments on. Elana was one of the few mages that showed him kindness there. In fact he was able to escape thanks to her, and she told him to meet her in Eldremist in order to help him find a cure for his curse. Schtuk promised to at least delay the killing of Elana for a few days

Eventually, the Little Guys were brought to the dungeon, and warned that they would not leave the town alive if Elana was not safe upon their return. Immediately things turned dire in the dungeon as the party walked into a room full of mimics. Smegenald tried to charm one, setting off the rest. using a "Sexy Mimic" as a distraction the party managed to escape, eventually finding the missing mages of Eldremist. The two survivors, Elana and [[Serra Nightshade]] (the sister of Selene), told the party that the mimics ate the rest of their party, one of them being Serra's husband found out by Smegenald while a terrible attempt of flirting.

With the two Eldremist mages, the party cleared the dungeon with ease, Schtuk found a new Lucky Knife, Shovel lined his pockets with money, Smegenald got to fail at flirting with the older sister of his biggest crush, and Sevrin got to show off his combat chops to the rest of the group. While in the dungeon, Elana expressed herself apologetically, telling the Little Guys of her remorse of her work with the Court of Mages. She had never intended on killing Skeech, and was nothing but a soldier in that fight. She had intended on using the battle as a way to disappear from the Court without them coming after her.

After fighting a tough Elder Oblex, the dungeon was cleared, arriving in the room where Serra performed the ritual. Shovel made Elana promise to make him a hat, and Smegenald got over the grudge quickly since Elana was a woman, but Schtuk remained vengeful against the mage that slain Skeech. Once out and meeting with Atticus, the elder introduced the party to a familiar face, [[Cronus Gaspar]]. Cronus revealed that in his spying on the Court of Mages, he has located a place in the [[Oasis of Vorag]] that a resurrection spell is being developed. The Little Guys decided to head there after taking some time to prepare in Eldremist.

Schtuk, however, crafted a plan. He wanted Elana to continue towards working for her penance, as simply making a hat for Shovel was not enough in his eyes. Thus, the Little Guys kidnapped her (and shit on her bed), under the guise of needing to bring a powerful mage to Selene Nightshade. The party left fast towards [[Thalgrimstahl]], with their quest to resurrect Skeech beginning.
